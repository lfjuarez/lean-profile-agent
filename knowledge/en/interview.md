## What I am looking for

Projects where there is a SIEM platform to scale or migrate (Splunk or Elastic), detections to build or measure against MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS, automation with SOAR or with AI agents, or a SOC team to train. I am especially interested in regulated environments (banking, insurance, energy) and in problems where the before and after can be measured: detection coverage, false positives, triage time, platform cost.

## Strengths

I combine three things that are rarely found in one person: SIEM platform engineering at scale (multi-site clusters, SmartStore, petabytes of events, 300+ sources), detection engineering (correlation searches mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, UEBA, SOAR playbooks) and AI applied to security operations (agents and MCP servers that migrated and validated 2,000+ rules). On top of that, Splunk's highest certification, authorization as an official instructor and a Master's in Data Science, plus the experience of 15 clients in regulated sectors with PCI DSS, ISO 27001 and BCRA audits. I explain technical matters in plain language, in writing and live, in Spanish and English.

## How I work under pressure and during incidents

With method and in stages. At CyberStone I gave on-demand support during incidents: threat hunting and forensic analysis on the platform, and scripts and automations to speed up troubleshooting and investigations. When the change is in production, I make it in stages with a way back and with a human approval gate for medium-confidence cases, as in the SOAR playbook rollout. I measure before automating (two weeks of alerts classified by hand before closing false positives automatically) and I prove disaster recovery with timed drills instead of assuming it works.

## How I work remotely

I work remotely from Buenos Aires, Argentina, on Latin America and Spain hours, with overlap with United States and European business hours. With every client I set fixed check-ins, work with clear written reports and defined deliverables, and record decisions in writing so there are no surprises. I speak native Spanish and English at full professional proficiency; I teach official Splunk courses in both languages.

## Why hire me

Because I deliver measurable results on real platforms, not prototypes: SIEM migrations in waves with old and new platforms in parallel, detection coverage measured technique by technique, playbooks that reduced false positives and triage time, and 2,000+ rules migrated with AI agents under automatic quality control. Every engagement ends with documentation and a hand-off session, so the client's team owns what was built. And if the team needs training, I am a Splunk Certified Instructor.
