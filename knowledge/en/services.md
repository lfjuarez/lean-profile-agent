## Splunk health check

Review of architecture, data ingestion, license usage, slow searches and security configuration of a Splunk platform, delivered as a written report with a scorecard, prioritized findings (evidence, impact, recommendation), capacity and performance analysis and a roadmap. Typical duration one to two weeks.

## Data onboarding and CIM normalization

Onboarding of a new data source (Universal Forwarder, HEC, syslog, DB Connect or REST), custom add-on and field extractions, CIM normalization and validation in Enterprise Security, priced per source.

## MITRE ATT&CK detections in Enterprise Security

Packages of correlation searches in SPL mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, with tuning against the client's real data and documentation for the SOC. Reference package: ten correlations.

## MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS coverage assessment

Inventory of every enabled rule, AI-assisted mapping to ATT&CK and ATLAS reviewed by hand, testing against the data actually collected, scored heat maps, Navigator layer file, prioritized gaps with a proposed detection for each, and a roadmap. See the detection coverage case study.

## SOAR playbooks

Design, implementation and testing of Splunk SOAR playbooks: integrations as reusable assets with least-privilege accounts, enrichment, verdict matrix with an analyst approval gate, containment and ticketing, with every action logged back to the notable. See the SOAR triage case study.

## SIEM migration assisted by AI

Inventory, conversion, quality control and MITRE coverage analysis when moving detection rules between SIEM platforms, using AI-agent pipelines (more than 2,000 rules migrated in production).

## Platform engineering and high-availability architecture

Multi-site Splunk clusters with HA and disaster recovery (Splunk Validated Architectures), SmartStore, data lifecycle design, source health monitoring, capacity models and failover runbooks that are actually drilled. Also Elastic SIEM in two regions. See the multi-site platform case study.

## AI assistants and MCP servers for security teams

Proofs of concept and projects: alert triage agents, enrichment, RAG over runbooks, assistants connected to Splunk, Elastic or Jira through the Model Context Protocol, and custom MCP servers. This site is itself an example: a cited chat over a knowledge base plus an MCP endpoint.

## Training

Live, custom training in English or Spanish with his own material: SPL from beginner to advanced, Splunk administration, SOC analyst skills (alert investigation, threat hunting) and detection engineering with MITRE ATT&CK, using the client's own data and cases. Typical format two or three live days with labs and material the team keeps. Official Splunk Education courses are delivered only through Splunk and its Authorized Learning Partners.

## Monthly support

Retainer with a bank of hours for troubleshooting, upgrades and questions.
