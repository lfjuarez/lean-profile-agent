## Summary

Leandro Federico Juárez is a Splunk Core Certified Consultant, Splunk Enterprise Certified Architect and Splunk Certified Instructor (authorized by Splunk for the Power User Fast Start track), with more than 12 years in IT and 6 years dedicated to SIEM, threat detection and security operations. He designs, implements and optimizes distributed and clustered Splunk and Elastic platforms (Cloud, on-premise and hybrid) for banking, insurance and energy; builds detections in Splunk Enterprise Security mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, SOAR playbooks and UEBA use cases; and automates detection engineering with AI agents (more than 2,000 detection rules migrated and validated). He holds a Master's degree in Data Science. He works remotely from Argentina on Latin America and Spain hours, with overlap with the United States and Europe.

## What I do

- Splunk and Elastic platform review and optimization: health checks with a prioritized plan, performance, license and capacity.
- High-availability architectures: multi-site indexer and search head clusters, SmartStore, disaster recovery, Splunk Validated Architectures.
- Data onboarding and normalization: Universal Forwarder, HEC, syslog, DB Connect, REST; CIM and ECS; hot-warm-cold-frozen lifecycle; source health monitoring.
- Detection engineering: Enterprise Security correlation searches mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, risk-based alerting, false-positive tuning, UEBA and SOAR playbooks.
- MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS coverage assessments (including AI and LLM application security).
- AI engineering for security: LLM agents, RAG and MCP integrations connected to Splunk, Elastic and Jira, including custom MCP servers.
- Live training for SOC teams: SPL, Splunk administration and detection engineering, in English or Spanish, with his own material.
