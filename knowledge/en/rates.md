## Availability

Available for freelance and contract work only, remote from Argentina, 20 to 30 hours per week. He can start a freelance or contract engagement immediately. Based in Argentina, working on Latin America and Spain hours with overlap with United States and European business hours. Languages: Spanish and English.

## Hourly rate

Reference hourly rate: USD 45 to 70 per hour, depending on the scope and the duration of the engagement. For clients in Europe, EUR 55 to 70 per hour. Final rates are agreed per project.

- Splunk health check with a prioritized plan: from 400 (full platform review from 1,200; extended review with roadmap and workshop from 2,500).
- Onboarding of one data source with CIM normalization: from 250.
- MITRE ATT&CK detections in Enterprise Security: from 450 for three detections; ten-detection package from 1,500.
- MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS coverage map: from 800.
- Live training, two hours in Spanish or English: from 200; full-day sessions from 900.
- AI assistant connected to a platform through MCP (Splunk, Elastic, Jira or a custom MCP server): from 1,000.

- SIEM migration assisted by AI: quoted per project after the rule inventory.

## How an engagement starts

Write with your Splunk or Elastic version, whether it is Cloud or on-premise, the daily data volume and what is not working. Leandro answers with how he would approach it, a scope and a quote. Every engagement ends with documentation and a hand-off session with the client's team.
