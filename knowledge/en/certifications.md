## Instructor authorization and scope

Leandro is a Splunk Certified Instructor since February 2025. He is authorized by Splunk to deliver the official Power User Fast Start track, in Spanish, which prepares students for the Splunk Core Certified Power User exam: searching, statistics, correlation, knowledge objects, field extractions and data models, with labs and assessments. He is in the authorization process for the System Administration and Data Administration tracks. Official Splunk Education material is delivered only through Splunk and its Authorized Learning Partners; outside that channel he delivers custom training with his own material (SPL, Splunk administration, SOC analyst skills, detection engineering).

## About SOAR

Leandro does not hold a SOAR certification. His Splunk SOAR knowledge is hands-on experience: he implemented Splunk SOAR, with automation and response playbooks, at a leading electricity distributor in Argentina, and designs playbooks and verdict models for SOC triage. SOAR appears in his profile as experience and a skill, never as a certification.
